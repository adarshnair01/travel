# Rajasthan 2022 — trip recap

From the [Wanderlog plan](https://wanderlog.com/plan/xdjxzovxlyzhiuup/trip-to-rajasthan).

1–11 Oct 2022. Wanderlog listed Bikaner → Jaisalmer → Jodhpur → Chittorgarh → Udaipur, then dumped most stops into Day 1 / 2 / 3 / 5. This page paces those places across all eleven calendar days. No hotels or flights in the plan. Chittorgarh is a city pin only.
