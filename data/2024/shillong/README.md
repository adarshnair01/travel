# Shillong & Meghalaya 2024 — recap

Imported from the [Wanderlog plan](https://wanderlog.com/plan/xxqwbefchlgstkrr/trip-to-shillong/shared).

14–19 Nov 2024. Wanderlog pinned GS Road Guwahati (14), Umiam Lake and the Cherry Blossom Festival at Bhoirymbong (15), Elephant Falls / Police Bazar / Laitkor / Cherrapunji (16), and Mawlynnong / Wah Umngot / Jaflong (17). Days 18 and 19 were empty; they are filled with the leftover Shillong loop (Mawphlang, Ward’s Lake, Laitlum) and the GAU fly-out.

No flights or hotels were saved on the plan. Stays are the usual split: Guwahati night 14, Shillong 15–19.
