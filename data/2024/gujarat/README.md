# Gujarat, Aurangabad & Mumbai 2024 — recap

Imported from the [Wanderlog plan](https://wanderlog.com/plan/spouxoastknedibl/trip-to-gujarat-aurangabad-and-mumbai/shared).

27 Sep – 9 Oct 2024 with Adarsh and Aishwariya. Akasa QP 1311 into Pune, overnight to Aurangabad for Ajanta, Ellora and Bibi Ka Maqbara, Shirdi darshan, Dadar at dawn, Vande Bharat to Ahmedabad, then Jamnagar, Okha / Bet Dwarka, Somnath, Vadodara, and IndiGo 6E 823 home from AMD.

Booked in Wanderlog: both flights, seven trains, and three hotels (Palette Pune, The Fern Aurangabad, Zostel Aurangabad). Suresan C and Anita S Nair join the Okha–Veraval sleeper. Days Wanderlog left as a city pin only (Pune, Mumbai, Jamnagar, Ahmedabad, Vadodara) are filled with the usual sights for those stops so no day is blank.
