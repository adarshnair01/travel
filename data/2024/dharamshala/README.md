# Dharamshala & Dalhousie 2024 — recap

Imported from the [Wanderlog plan](https://wanderlog.com/plan/gyvpadgeowjublvh/trip-to-dharamsala-and-dalhousie).

8–15 Jun 2024 with Adarsh, Aishwariya, Akash, mum and dad. Vande Bharat into Amb Andaura, Dalhousie and Khajjiar first, Dharamshala second, Vande Bharat home on the 15th.

**Stays (as we actually slept):** The Hosteller Khajjiar 9–11 Jun; a Dharamshala hotel 11–13 Jun; The Hosteller McLeodganj, Bhagsu 14–15 Jun (Wanderlog). Wanderlog had McLeodganj 8–11 — that listing is wrong; we were in Khajjiar those nights.

Wanderlog left every day empty. The day-by-day is the usual circuit for those dates: Dalhousie / Dainkund / Khajjiar, then McLeodganj, Bhagsu, Triund and Kangra Fort.
