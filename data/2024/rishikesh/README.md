# Rishikesh, Chakrata & Mussoorie 2024 — recap

Imported from the [Wanderlog plan](https://wanderlog.com/plan/lctrocvvsnsccelp/trip-to-rishikesh). Solo trip, Adarsh Nair, 20–27 Nov 2024.

**Booked:** Akasa QP 1359 BLR → DEL; Shatabdi 12017 NDLS → HW; five Hosteller stays (Delhi, Rishikesh Ganges, Chakrata, Mussoorie Mall Road, Rishikesh Ganges again); DDN Kota AC Exp 12402 HW → NZM; IndiGo 6E 2217 DEL → MAA and 6E 6073 MAA → CNN.

Wanderlog left every day empty. The day-by-day follows the booked loop: Delhi night, one night in Rishikesh, Chakrata, two nights Mussoorie, two nights back in Rishikesh, overnight train and the Chennai connection home.

Lakshman Jhula was under construction — Wanderlog notes a boat from the taxi stand to The Hosteller Rishikesh, Ganges.
