# Bali, KL & Bangkok 2024 — recap

Flights and hotels from the [Wanderlog plan](https://wanderlog.com/plan/sohnsttpcasiezpd/trip-to-bali-kuala-lumpur-and-more/shared); the Bali day plans are ours, since Wanderlog never held a sightseeing list.

9–26 Aug 2024 with Adarsh and Aishwariya. Overnight AirAsia into Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia Airlines to Penang, then KL → Denpasar.

Bali, matched to the hotel each night: Kuta and Seminyak from Luminor Legian, Tanah Lot and Canggu, Tegallalang and Taro, Ubud (Tirta Empul, Monkey Forest, Campuhan), Uluwatu and the Kecak, then Nusa Dua and the Bukit beaches. Nusa Penida 20–22 Aug — Kelingking, Broken Beach, Angel's Billabong, Manta Point, Diamond and Atuh — booked outside Wanderlog, which is why the plan shows a gap after Mercure.

Indonesia AirAsia to Bangkok on the 23rd, three nights at ibis Styles Silom, Thai Lion Air home. The Penang and Bangkok days still have no stops recorded.
