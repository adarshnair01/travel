Eight days for two, 25 October to 1 November 2025. Malaysia Airlines leaves Bengaluru at 00:40 on the 25th, reaches Kuala Lumpur at 07:30, and continues at 09:15 to Kota Kinabalu at 11:55. The return on 1 November leaves Kota Kinabalu at 16:05, reaches Kuala Lumpur at 18:45, and leaves again at 21:30 to land in Bengaluru at 23:30. The fare for both of us, for the whole journey, is ₹48,060. Flight numbers were not saved.

The hotel is ibis Styles Kota Kinabalu Inanam, from the 25th to the 1st. The conference is at Sabah International Convention Centre: the venue on the morning of the 26th, the paper on the 27th, and a gala dinner with the attendees and a cultural show on the 28th.

The other days are the city and the two outings. Arrival evening is Padang Merdeka and Gaya Street. After the venue, Tanjung Aru Beach. Then a ferry from Jesselton Point to Manukan and Mamutik in Tunku Abdul Rahman Park, a highland day at Desa Dairy Farm and the rafflesia in Kundasang, Mari Mari Cultural Village and Likas, and on the last morning Sri Pasupathinath Alayam in Bukit Padang and Imago before the flight. Hours for those stops were not recorded, so they are paced around the conference.
