Two days from Bengaluru to Tirupati. On 15 May a cab took us to Peenya, where the BlaBlaCar ride began. The road out of Bengaluru had beautiful scenery beside it. VIP darshan was booked for that day, and the queue took around three to four hours. We came back on 16 May by 17210 Seshadri Express, Second AC, coach A2, two of us, fare ₹1,685.
