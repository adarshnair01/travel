Four days, two of us, three beds. The only pins were the three towns — Rishikesh, Lansdowne, Dehradun — and the hotels: The Hosteller Rishikesh, Ganges on the 7th, The Hosteller Lansdowne on the 8th, Le Roi beside Dehradun station on the 9th. The days below pace those three towns instead of stacking them: Ganga aarti on arrival night, Beatles Ashram then the Kotdwar climb, a Lansdowne morning, a Dehradun evening, Robber’s Cave before checkout.
