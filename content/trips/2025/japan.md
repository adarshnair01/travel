Thirteen days for two. Japan Airlines JL 754 leaves Kempegowda at 02:40 on 10 April and lands at Narita at 14:15. Tokyo holds the first six nights — APA Hotel Roppongi Six on two back-to-back bookings, then one night at Sotetsu Fresa Inn Tokyo Kyobashi. The saved days are Tokyo Tower, Akihabara, Nakano Broadway, teamLab Planets, a Fuji day (the mountain, Chureito Pagoda, Fuji-Q Highland), Nikko, then Shinjuku and Shibuya. Kyoto is Almont Hotel from the 16th to the 18th, with Philosopher’s Path, Kinkaku-ji, Nara Park, Kiyomizu-dera, Nishiki Market and Fushimi Inari. Osaka is Richmond Hotel Namba Daikokucho from the 18th to the 22nd: Dotonbori, the Expo 2025 monument pin, Kaiyukan, Abeno Harukas, Osaka Castle Park, Umeda Sky and Universal Studios Japan. Home on the 22nd is JL 3006 from Itami to Narita, then JL 753, landing Bengaluru at 00:50 on the 23rd. All three flights share confirmation WS55OM.
