Eight days alone after Shillong. Dawn Akasa into Delhi, one night at The Hosteller Delhi, then the 06:45 Shatabdi to Haridwar and a bus-and-boat hop to The Hosteller Rishikesh, Ganges — Lakshman Jhula was under construction. Next morning west: Rishikesh → Dehradun ISBT → Vikasnagar → Chakrata, two nights on Mussoorie Mall Road, two nights back on the Ganga, then the 00:14 DDN Kota AC Express into Nizamuddin and IndiGo home via Chennai to Kannur.
