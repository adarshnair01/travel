Eighteen days in August 2024 with Aishwariya. AirAsia overnight Bengaluru → Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia Airlines into Penang, then KL again and AirAsia to Denpasar. Ten days across Bali — Kuta and Legian, Tanah Lot, Canggu, the Tegallalang terraces, Ubud, Uluwatu and Nusa Dua — then three days on Nusa Penida before Bangkok and the flight home. Three countries, seven hotels and six flights in eighteen days.
