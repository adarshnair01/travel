Eleven calendar days with Aishwariya. Finnair AY 122 into Helsinki on Christmas Day, two nights at Scandic Helsinki Hub, then Tallink to Tallinn for two nights at Hyatt Place by the harbour. Back across the Gulf on the 29th, overnight VR to Rovaniemi for a polar-night day, overnight VR south, then the Silja Line New Year’s cruise from Olympia Terminal into Stockholm on 1 Jan. Three nights at Scandic Go on Sankt Eriksgatan, Norwegian D8 2603 ARN → HEL on the 4th, and Finnair AY 121 home after dusk.
