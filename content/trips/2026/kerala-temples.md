A short pilgrimage with spouse and in-laws, out of Kannur and home again. On 7 March the stops are Guruvayur Sri Krishna Temple and Ambalappuzha Sree Krishna Swamy Temple; on 8 March, Mannarasala Sree Nagaraja Temple, in its grove at Haripad, before the return to Kannur. Ambalapuzha and Mannarsala are the spellings we started from; these pages use Ambalappuzha and Mannarasala. The order is the sensible one south, then home — how the distance was covered, and where the night was spent, was not recorded.
