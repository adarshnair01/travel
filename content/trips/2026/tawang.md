Eleven days in a cab. Air India Express from Bengaluru into Guwahati via Jaipur on the 15th, one night in the Railway ORH, then the Bhairabkund road into Arunachal — Balemu checkpoint, a viewpoint by Gyuto Monastery, a hot spring in the tarmac just before Rupa. Dirang, Sela Lake, a frozen Nuranang Chu with a yak in it, Jang Falls, the war memorial under army lights. Bumla and Sangetsar on our anniversary in a cab that was allowed on that road. Khaar Hotel for the monastery view, the Sela tunnel home, a Bhutan petrol pump where the rupee was still the rupee, then Umananda, Kamakhya on Basant Panchami, a trade fair, kites on the Brahmaputra, and a train to Silchar before the Kolkata layover home.
