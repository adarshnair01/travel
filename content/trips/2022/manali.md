Thirteen days in June 2022. AirAsia into Delhi for two office days in Gurgaon, a Delhi sightseeing afternoon, Kalka Shatabdi to Chandigarh and an overnight bus that ran two hours late into Manali. Hadimba, Solang, Rohtang La and the Spiti road, Manikaran and the trek to Kheerganga, then the Golden Temple and Wagah in Amritsar. Home on the night flight of the 28th.
