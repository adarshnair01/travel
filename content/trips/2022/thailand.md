Six days with Aishwariya across southern Thailand. We flew through Bangkok to Krabi, bargained locally for Maya Bay and Phi Phi, crossed by road to Phuket, spent a day in the old town and at Carnival Magic, took a private boat to Banana Island, then finished among Bangkok’s markets, wats and the Grand Palace before flying home.
