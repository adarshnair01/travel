A thirteen-day August 2022 road trip from A.L.Y. Fort House in Amrutahalli. Day one is Shivanasamudra and Bandipur into Wayanad. Day two drops through Chembra and Banasura to Kannur home. Then Athirappilly, Munnar, Idukki and Vagamon, a Kumarakom boat, Varkala and Trivandrum, Madurai, Rameswaram and Dhanushkodi, Pondicherry, Chennai–Sriharikota–Tirupati, and back to Bangalore. One hotel is booked. Some day headings do not match the pins under them.
