An eleven-day Rajasthan loop in October 2022 — Bikaner, Jaisalmer, Jodhpur, Chittorgarh and Udaipur. Forts every second morning, two nights out by the dunes at Sam, and the last stretch on the lakes at Udaipur. The days below are paced by city and drive time across 1–11 Oct.
