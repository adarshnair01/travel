Privacy policy

Last updated November 9, 2026.

This site (https://adarshnair.online/travel/) is run by Adarsh Nair. It has no accounts and doesn't ask you for personal information.

Analytics

I use Google Analytics 4 to understand which stories are read and how people find them. It uses cookies and collects things like pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser, and clicks on outbound links. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Advertising

This site may use Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalised ads in Google Ads Settingsor opt out of third-party vendors at aboutads.info.

Affiliate links

Some links to hotels, tours and gear are affiliate links (Amazon Associates, and partners through Travelpayouts). If you book or buy through one, the partner may set a cookie so the blog can earn a small commission, at no extra cost to you. A Travelpayouts script on these pages tracks those links.

Likes and shares

When you tap Like or Love, or share a story, your browser adds one to an anonymous counter for that story, kept by the free Abacus counting service. No name, cookie or account is involved. Your browser remembers locally which stories you've reacted to, so each one counts only once.

Your preferences

The light/dark theme choice is stored in your browser's local storage and never sent anywhere.
